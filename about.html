How scoring works

The preserved TSR engine measures playable depth in minutes against orbit-cost growth at levels 6, 10, 14 and 18. Material blind jumps above 60% subtract 4 points; above 100% subtract 8. Fees inform commentary, not the numeric score. A score is not a percentage, profit prediction or recommendation to enter.

Weekly and series curves

Weekly events generally target a practical evening finish; series events can allow longer play. The current official implementation chooses Tier 1 for total buy-in ≤ $300 and Tier 2 above $300. Event type does not override that rule.

Tier 1 base bands: A ≥48, B ≥40, C ≥32, D ≥24. Tier 2: A ≥85, B ≥65, C ≥50, D ≥38. Below D is F. Bands split into thirds for minus/plain/plus; top modifier spans end at 56 and 105. Scores are uncapped.

SCO-001: event-type versus buy-in curve selection is pending Sham’s decision. Compare scores within the same curve.
