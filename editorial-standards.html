Editorial standards

Show the source.
Own the correction.

Proposed policy — owner approval needed before publication.

Reporting and opinion

Distinguish confirmed information, attributed allegations and the author’s analysis. Preserve original publication dates and add dated updates when facts change.

Sources and corrections

Link to primary sources where available. Material errors should receive a visible correction explaining what changed and when. The correction contact route still needs to be selected.

Commercial relationships

Disclose sponsorship, advertising, hosted-game relationships and relevant conflicts alongside affected coverage. The final funding and sponsorship policy needs approval.

Grades and freshness

Keep structure analysis separate from schedule verification. Missing information must remain visible; a historical record cannot imply a room is open or a game is running now.
