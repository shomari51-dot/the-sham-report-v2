The TSR Score

Understand the grade.

A structure assessment built for Texas poker players.

What it considers

The calculator reads the full blind and ante progression, starting chips and playable time. It also reports entry fees and can account for add-on chips and scheduled changes to level length.

Read the score in context

The current scoring engine uses tier-specific letter bands. The raw score is not a percentage or a score out of 100. A score and its letter must be interpreted within the same tier.

Complete information comes first

A calculation needs at least 18 blind levels. Missing structures are labelled rather than invented. Historic grade records without structure can display the published score but cannot be fully recalculated.

What it cannot tell you

A grade does not verify an event’s current schedule, guarantee terms, operating status or seat availability. It cannot predict profit.
