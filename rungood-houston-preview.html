RunGood Poker Series Hits Houston This Week

By Shomari Williams  |  October 12, 2026

For the first time ever, the RunGood Poker Series is coming to Houston. The RGPS Golden Expedition runs October 12-18, exclusively at Champions Club (6440 W Sam Houston Pkwy S, Houston), with $510K in published guarantees and 11 championship rings up for grabs. The headliner is a $600 Main Event with a $250,000 guarantee.

Champions Club is offering a tournament room rate starting at $79 a night for players who want to stay and play. Club membership is required to play, and the structure sheet lists the fee portion of each buy-in as a "facility access fee."

Where the value is

The Main Event is the best-priced event on the schedule, with a 16.7% fee on a $600 buy-in, a 60,000 stack, and 30-minute levels. The $400 events (Monster Stack, PowerStack, Last Chance) run 17.5%, and the $300 events run 20%. If you're picking one event this week, the Main gives you the most structure for your money.

TSR Score grades

Graded from the Champions Club structure sheets with the TSR Score calculator.

  • Monster Stack Opener ($400, 50,000 stack, 30-minute levels): TSR 69.8 (B−). 17.5% fee, with playable depth through level 19.
  • RunGood Main Event ($600, 60,000 stack, 30-minute levels): TSR 66.0 (B−). 16.7% fee. Same letter as the opener, slightly lower score, and the best fee of the three.
  • PokerNews PowerStack ($400, 40,000 stack, 30-minute levels): TSR 46.6 (D+). 17.5% fee. Same buy-in as the Monster Stack, but the 40,000 stack and faster blind climb cut depth to level 15.

Fee percentage still favors the Main, but structure favors the Monster Stack. The PowerStack is the softest of the three on skill depth.

Sources: RunGood event page, Champions Club structure sheets (PDF), PokerNews preview, and the Champions Club Texas email announcement (Oct 10, 2026).
