One level per line. Small blind, big blind, then the big blind ante. Any separator works — slashes, commas, or spaces.

100 / 200 / 200   100,200,200   6  100 200 200

Leading level numbers are detected and ignored, so you can paste a structure sheet straight in. No ante? Leave it off: 100 / 200. Lines containing the word "break" are skipped.

If the level length varies from level to level, add it as a fourth number — 100 / 200 / 200 / 30 — on every line. For a single change partway through, the Level Length Change field above is easier.

The score samples levels 6, 10, 14 and 18, so at least 18 levels are required.

What this grade means for you

How TSR Score Works

TSR Score is an objective grading system. It reads starting stack, blind structure, level length and fees, and returns an unbiased grade before you put money on the table.

Depth

Measures how long your stack lasts relative to the blinds.

Acceleration

Penalises fast blind increases that shorten playability.

Vig

Calculates total fees as a percentage of the buy-in.

Blind Jumps

Detects and penalises uneven structures that spike too hard.
