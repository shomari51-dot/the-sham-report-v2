Choctaw Circuit Is Live: The DFW Grinder's Guide to Durant

The WSOP Circuit is in Durant Oct 28 through Nov 9: 18 ring events, a $250K Gargantuan and a $1,700 Main Event with a $1M guarantee, per WSOP.com. Here's the official schedule, the operator's fees, and what July's numbers tell you.

By Shomari Williams · October 29, 2026

The WSOP Circuit is back in Durant, which makes it the closest ring stop for anyone in DFW. Choctaw Casino Resort is hosting Oct 28 through Nov 9. WSOP.com lists it as a "SUPER CIRCUIT," with online qualifiers on GGPoker and ClubGG.

The official WSOP.com schedule has 18 ring events. Event numbers in this guide are WSOP.com's. Poker Executives, the tournament operator at Choctaw, numbers its own schedule differently because it also counts nightlies and satellites; we show those as "operator sheet #" for reference only. Fees are not on WSOP.com; the fee figures below come from the operator's structure sheets.

What WSOP.com has confirmed

  • Dates and venue: Oct 28 – Nov 9, 2026, Choctaw Casino Resort, Durant, OK. (stop page (opens in new tab))
  • 18 ring events, numbered #1–#18 on the official schedule (opens in new tab).
  • Event #3, Gargantuan: $400 NLH, $250K GTD, Flights A–E (Oct 29 4 PM; Oct 30 11 AM & 4 PM; Oct 31 11 AM & 4 PM), 20,000 chips, late reg until the start of Level 9, unlimited re-entry. (Operator sheet #5.)
  • Event #14, Main Event: $1,700 NLH, $1M GTD, Flight A Fri Nov 6 and Flight B Sat Nov 7, both 11 AM, 40,000 chips, 40/60-minute clock, late reg until the start of Level 13. (Operator sheet #34.)
  • Event #13, Mini Main: $1,100 NLH, $200K GTD, Flights Thu Nov 5 at 11 AM and 5 PM, 30,000 chips. (Operator sheet #29.)
  • Other examples: #1 Monster Stack $400 (40,000); #2 Double Stack Freezeout $500 (40,000); #4 Ladies $300; #5 NLH $400 (25,000, 20-min levels); #7 Seniors $50K GTD $300; #18 The Finale $400.
  • Not on WSOP.com: admin fees, the $130 Nightlies and the Mega satellites, and Main Event Day 2/Day 3 times. Those come from the operator only.

Operator schedule with fees (Poker Executives; WSOP.com numbers added)

Poker Executives, which runs tournaments at Choctaw, has posted the full 2026 October schedule and a structure sheet for every numbered event on its Durant page (pokerexecutives.com/durant (opens in new tab)). The sheet is titled "WSOP 18 Rings Circuit Event" and lists 43 numbered events from Oct 28 through the Main Event's Day 3 on Nov 9.

The highlights for DFW players:

  • Main Event (WSOP.com #14; operator sheet #34): $1,700 ($1,515 to the prize pool, $185 admin fee). Flight A Fri Nov 6 and Flight B Sat Nov 7, both 11 AM. 40,000 chips, 40/60-minute clock, three re-entries in Flight A and two in Flight B (per WSOP.com), registration open until the start of level 13. Day 2 Sun Nov 8 at noon, Day 3 Mon Nov 9 at noon. WSOP.com lists it as "$1M GTD."
  • Mini Main (WSOP.com #13; operator sheet #29): $1,100 ($975 / $125), $200,000 per the schedule. Flights Thu Nov 5 at 11 AM and 5 PM, 30,000 chips, unlimited re-entry.
  • Gargantuan (WSOP.com #3; operator sheet #5): $400 ($330 / $70), $250,000. Five flights Oct 29–31, 20,000 chips, unlimited re-entry, restart Sun Nov 1 at noon.
  • Kickoff Monster Stack (WSOP.com #1; operator sheet #1): $400 ($330 / $70), Wed Oct 28 at 11 AM and 4 PM, 40,000 chips, unlimited re-entry.
  • Seniors (WSOP.com #7; operator sheet #16): $300, $50K GTD (WSOP.com), Mon Nov 2 at 10 AM.
  • Every ring event on the sheets awards a "non-refundable, non-transferable 5K Package to Bahamas in December" to the winner, per the structure sheets.
  • Satellites: $200 "Mega for Main Event" seat satellites run most nights at 8 PM, with extra flights Nov 5–7. For every $1,700 in the prize pool, one Main Event buy-in is awarded. There are $130 "Mega for Mini Main" satellites on Nov 5 at 9 AM and 3 PM.
  • Nightly $130 NLH at 7 PM ($98 / $32), 20,000 chips, 20-minute levels.

Source: Poker Executives (tournament operator), retrieved Oct 28, 2026. The "WSOP.com #" column is from the official WSOP.com schedule (Oct 29); rows marked "not on WSOP.com" are non-ring nightlies and satellites listed only by the operator. Buy-ins, dates, times, stacks, late reg and re-entry on the 18 ring events follow WSOP.com.

How July went (WSOP.com official results, Jul 15–27, 2026)

  • Main Event: $1,700, 1,117 entries, $1,555,875 prize pool. Won by Carson Wieland.
  • Mini Main: $1,100, 400 entries, $351,580 prize pool. Won by Maurice Hawkins.
  • Gargantuan: $400, 2,303 entries, $584,520 prize pool, against a $300K guarantee. Won by Jacob Arguello.
  • Seniors: $300, 783 entries.

July's Main beat this stop's $1M guarantee (per WSOP.com) by more than half a million dollars. The Gargantuan nearly doubled its guarantee. This time the Gargantuan guarantee is $250K, down from $300K. (July results (opens in new tab))

Who runs the floor

Poker Executives says on its own site that it handles tournament management at Choctaw Durant. Its Durant page hosts the structure sheets and schedule for this stop (pokerexecutives.com/durant (opens in new tab)). The sheets themselves carry Choctaw Casino's disclaimer, and the schedule is titled "WSOP 18 Rings Circuit Event." No tournament director is named.
